# Shopmate AI Store Knowledge Base

## Discounts

No general discount is currently confirmed for the store. The chatbot must not promise a discount that is not listed in this file.

## End-of-season sale

No end-of-season sale date is currently scheduled. A date will be added here when the store confirms one.

## Return policy

Customers may request a return within 30 days of delivery for unworn clothing with original tags attached. The item must be in resalable condition. Return eligibility is subject to review; this proof of concept does not process returns.

## Shipping

Standard delivery is estimated at 3-5 business days after an order is placed. Shipping cost information is not available in this knowledge base. This proof of concept does not connect to a shipping provider.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the website require an account?** No. Login and registration are not required for this proof of concept.

**Can I pay for an order on the website?** No. The proof of concept calculates a cart invoice but does not process payment.

**Can I ask about a store policy not listed here?** The chatbot must clearly say that the requested information is not available rather than inventing an answer.
